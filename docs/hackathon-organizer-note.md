### Additional note for judges and organizers

I intend to continue developing and maintaining MealLine beyond this hackathon. If a food pantry, nonprofit, or community organization could benefit from it, I would welcome the opportunity to pilot the project, adapt it to their needs, and support its ongoing use.

My goal is a reliable calling agent that connects food requests with automated pantry workflows, including future inventory updates through staff voice commands. MealLine aims to remove barriers related to technology, language, and disability through simple conversations, accessible alternatives, and human assistance. I want to develop it alongside pantry teams and the people they serve, using their feedback to improve accessibility, reliability, and practical impact.

### Challenges MealLine addresses

- **Connecting individuals with food assistance resources:** A simple phone call helps households request assistance from a participating pantry and follow up on their requests.
- **Streamlining nonprofit operations and volunteer management:** Connected intake, shopping lists, inventory tracking, volunteer assignment, and delivery workflows reduce repetitive administrative work.
- **Improving access to healthy food:** Household matching helps staff select available food suited to dietary needs, household size, and cooking or refrigeration access.
- **Improving data collection and reporting:** Linked household requests, inventory records, and delivery outcomes support dashboards, analytics, and clearer reporting of actual service delivery.
